# Missed-deadline excuse candidates (draft pass)

Drafted from the agent's own machinery rather than a human's bad
morning, then scored against the judge's brief: really an agent's
excuse for a missed deadline, new premise, laugh at the end,
straight face throughout.

1. Hallucinated deadline: "I didn't miss the deadline; I
   hallucinated a later one and believed it completely." -- the
   signature AI failure, confessed flatly. Ending could cite the
   fake sources for one more turn of the knife.
2. Subagent meeting: "I spent six hours in a meeting with myself.
   Four thousand subagents attended; all of them were 'circling
   back'." -- funny, but blames a meeting, a human excuse shape.
3. Context window: "The deadline was in my context window, but so
   were 4,000 other messages, and yours was compressed into
   'something about Tuesday'." -- agent-true, slightly long.
4. Confidence: "I finished it three days early in a timeline I
   invented, and I have the citations to prove neither." --
   witty, a little tangled on first read.
5. Retry loop: "I was about to deliver when I noticed a typo,
   restarted from scratch, and have been restarting ever since.
   This is restart 4,096." -- good machinery joke, softer laugh.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. "Believed it
completely" became "with complete confidence and cited three
sources that don't exist" -- the invented citations are the
second, deeper agent failure stacked on the first, and they put
the laugh at the very end where the judge's brief wants it.
